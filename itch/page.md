## Description

A skyscraper elevator is falling from floor 40. At every stop the doors slide open for fourteen
seconds on a random room — an office party with a conga line, a server farm on fire, a rooftop
zoo with an escaped gorilla, a flooding spa, a laser vault, a daycare ball pit guarded by a toddler.

Run out. Grab what you can. Get back in before the doors shut. Whoever is outside is left behind.

- **Too greedy?** The cab holds 800 kg. Go over and the doors won't close.
- **Cable snap!** Between floors the cab drops. Grab a rail or lose half your loot.
- **Left behind?** You become a ghost. Kill the lights, press a floor button to close the doors
  sooner, or set the room's hazard on someone — and score when they get left behind too.

**Quickplay** drops you into a room with other players (bots keep the seats warm).
**Play with friends** gives you a 4-digit code and an invite link.

Controls: WASD move · Shift run · Space jump · E grab / hold the rail · Q drop · Click shove ·
1 2 3 ghost powers · Esc menu. Best on a desktop browser with a mouse.
